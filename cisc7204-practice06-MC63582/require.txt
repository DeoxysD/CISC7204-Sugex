Learning Activity for Practice 06: Identifying Misleading Visual(s)

REMARK: This learning activity is supposed be done and submitted in your HOME UMMoodle site labelled with one of your CLASS letter (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, K, L, or M). It should NOT be done and submitted in the MAIN UMMoodle site of CISC7204.

Instructions

In this Practice activity,  you will have to locate and identify a visual that displays misleading information. You will interpret the features of the visual in order to identify the mechanism(s) that is/are used by the "encoder" to mislead the "decoder." For each mechanism that you identify, you will explain how it was used to mislead. You are free to complete this activity with the help of an AI assistant (or chatbot) of your choice.

Action Steps

06.01 Locate an example of a misleading visual that uses one or more of the mechanisms for misleading that Cairo outlines in his work Graphics Lies, Misleading Visuals: hiding relevant data, displaying too much data and obscuring reality, and distorting data through graphic forms.
06.02 Upload an image of this visual in the personal forum by creating a thread named "My Mislaeding Visual Identified", with some related annotations of your findings.
06.03 Provide, in APA style, a citation link and a reference to the source of the visual (e.g., the article in which the visual appears) in the thread created in 06.02.
06.04 Identify and explain concisely the misleading mechanism(s) that is/are used in the visual in the thread created in 06.02.
Hint - You may want to use google or bing image search with a parameter such as "information visualization", or "scientific poster chart" or similar in order to find visualizations to critique. You might also want to consider looking on the subreddits dataisbeautiful or dataisugly.

For each of the above steps, you may instruct an AI assistant for a particular domain and then extend help to your work via a PROMPT-based conversation: You will reflect on what initially drew you to the conversation and what problems or questions you need help to accomplish.
